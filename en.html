All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the worlds. May blessings and peace be upon the final Prophet and Messenger, our master Muhammad, and upon all his family and companions.

To proceed:

This is a Sharia ruqyah recited by Mufti Junaid. It is beneficial and tried for nullifying magic, the evil eye and envy, and very beneficial for driving devils and jinn away from a person, a home or any place you are in.

How to know whether you are affected by magic, possession or the evil eye:

Listen to this Sharia ruqyah for just a quarter of an hour at a time when you are alert. Observe whether you feel these signs and whether they appear:

If the answer is yes, you are affected and should treat yourself with this Sharia ruqyah. If no sign appears, this means that your illness is medical rather than spiritual.

The signs are:

Heaviness in the shoulders, swelling of the head, repeated yawning, drowsiness with sudden sleep, an urge to vomit, increased pain at the affected site, diarrhoea, belching, aversion to hearing ruqyah, wanting to scream, or wanting to escape from hearing ruqyah.

If one or more of these signs appears, you should treat yourself with this Sharia ruqyah.

How to use it:

1. Perform ablution and sit at a time when you are alert.

2. Listen to this Sharia ruqyah twice in 24 hours.

3. Listen attentively, not absent-mindedly.

4. Do not listen while occupied with other work.

5. Be patient and persevere while listening.

6. Any harm you feel is in fact from devils, so do not interrupt the recitation.

7. Remember that victory requires patience.

8. Be certain that Allah Almighty alone is the Healer.

9. Continuing the treatment is the treatment.

10. Care for the sick person through the prayer of need, supplication and charity.
